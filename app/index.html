⚡ The 6 to internalize

  • 1Engine before leverage. Reserves + stable cash flow first; holding power beats equity.
  • 2Stay under your concentration ceiling (RE + leverage as % of net worth, incl. home equity).
  • 3Underwrite your own numbers — reassessed taxes + real insurance, never the seller's pro forma.
  • 4Risk-adjusted return, not the headline cap rate. A 9% cap is usually a C/D headache.
  • 5Insurance is the first protection layer — the LLC is a backstop, and a personal guarantee bypasses it.
  • 6Stress-test for correlated shocks, not one variable at a time.
