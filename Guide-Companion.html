⚡ The 6 things to internalize

  • 1 Engine before leverage. Reserves + stable cash flow first; holding power beats equity.
  • 2 Stay under your concentration ceiling. RE + leverage as a share of net worth (count home equity).
  • 3 Underwrite your own numbers — never the seller's. Reassessed taxes + real insurance.
  • 4 Chase risk-adjusted return, not the headline cap rate. A 9% cap is usually a C/D headache.
  • 5 Insurance is the first asset-protection layer — the LLC is a backstop, and a personal guarantee bypasses it.
  • 6 Stress-test for correlated shocks, not one variable at a time.
